Data Protection Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026
App: VERIN (Android)

This data protection policy explains which personal data are processed when you use the VERIN app, and for what purposes. VERIN helps you plan daily commitments, track progress, and calculate a discipline score.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Nils Pachaly
Email: nils.pachaly@gmail.com

2. What data are processed?

Depending on how you use the app, the following data may be processed:

  • Account data: email address and password (passwords are not stored in plain text; authentication is handled by the auth service).
  • App content: commitments you create (titles, categories, goals, deadlines, recurrence), progress and check-in entries, and the resulting daily and score history.
  • Technical session data: authentication tokens used to keep you signed in on the device.

VERIN does not request contacts, location, camera/microphone permissions, or advertising IDs for tracking. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used in the described version of the app.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Providing and operating the app’s features
  • Registration, sign-in, and account security
  • Storing and syncing your commitments and history across devices
  • Handling support and data protection requests

4. Legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract / pre-contractual measures) – for use of the account and app features
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) – e.g. secure operation and abuse prevention, where necessary
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) – if you voluntarily provide additional information or give consent

5. Storage on the device

App data may be cached locally on your device (e.g. a commitments cache), and session information may be stored in a protected area of the operating system so you remain signed in.

6. Cloud storage / processors

When cloud sync is enabled, account data and app content are processed via Supabase (authentication and database). Data are stored in a record scoped to your user account.

Provider: Supabase Inc. or affiliated entities. Details on the provider’s data protection practices are available in its documentation and privacy policy. Where required, a data processing agreement is or will be in place with the provider.

7. Sharing with third parties

Personal data are shared with third parties only where necessary to perform the contract (e.g. hosting/auth via Supabase), where you have consented, or where we are legally obliged to do so. User data are not sold.

8. Retention

  • Account data and synced app content are stored for as long as your account exists and the data are needed to provide the app.
  • Commitment and daily history are generally kept in the app on an ongoing basis so progress remains visible over time, until you delete data or the account.
  • After account deletion or upon request, we delete or anonymize the related personal data unless legal retention obligations require otherwise.

9. Delete account

VERIN – Delete account

To delete your VERIN account and all associated data, email nils.pachaly@gmail.com from the email address of your VERIN account.

This includes, among other things, your account, commitments, check-ins, and score history.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have, in particular, the following rights:

  • Access to the personal data we process
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future

To exercise your rights, email the contact address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Children

VERIN is not directed at children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted personal data without consent, please contact us.

12. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures (e.g. encrypted transmission via HTTPS, protected session storage on the device, access-restricted cloud data). Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this data protection policy if the app, the law, or our processing changes. The current version is always available at this URL; the “Last updated” date is revised when changes are made.
